## Datasets

* `Applied-Statistics/csv files/` — course datasets (cars, houses, penguins,
  snow petrels, chocolate, MIGA projects, and more).
* `Applied-Statistics/data/external/` — five public datasets cached from
  statsci.org: `insulgas`, `mazdas`, `sleep`, `punting`, `hanford`.
* `Numerical-Analysis-Lab/data/` — climate CSVs, 41 face images, four audio
  recordings.
* `CS_5320/csv/` — chocolate bars, courses, students, enrollment.
* `ECON284/Data/`, `ECON411/`, `mobile-RPG-observation/` — economics and
  thesis datasets.
* `InfoNetworks/pythonCoauthorship/ps1data.txt` — 123k-line bibliography for
  the coauthorship graph (12 MB; shared by both entry points).
* `exercises/` — synthetic authors/papers tables plus cached API responses.

Datasets that were **not** included with the course are called out in the
relevant README; the most important one is the "roots" data used by several
`Applied-Statistics` effect-size scripts.

Source: ACLU – Know Your Rights: Protesters’ Rights

The First Amendment protects your right to assemble and express your views through protest. Law enforcement cannot require permits for small groups that do not block traffic or access to buildings.

If you are stopped by police, stay calm and do not argue. Ask if you are free to go. If the officer says you are under arrest, you have the right to remain silent and the right to a lawyer. Do not resist physically, even if you believe your rights are being violated.

If you think your rights were violated, write down everything you remember, including officers’ names and badge numbers, and contact a lawyer. File a written complaint with the appropriate agency.

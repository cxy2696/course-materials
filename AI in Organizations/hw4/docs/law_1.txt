Source: Cornell Law School Legal Information Institute – Landlord and Tenant

A landlord is a person or business that rents real estate, including land, houses, apartments, or rooms, to another person known as a tenant. The landlord-tenant relationship is founded on duties that are fixed by law and by contract.

Most states have enacted laws governing the rights and duties of landlords and tenants. For example, landlords generally have a duty to provide habitable premises and may not evict tenants without proper legal procedures. Tenants, in turn, have a duty to pay rent and to avoid damaging the property.

Many states and localities have agencies that handle landlord-tenant complaints and provide information about rental rights. Tenants and landlords should consult local statutes or legal aid organizations for specific guidance.

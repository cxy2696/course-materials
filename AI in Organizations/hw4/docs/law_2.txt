Source: ACLU – Know Your Rights: Students’ Rights in School

Students do not lose their constitutional rights when they enter the schoolhouse door, but schools can set rules to keep students safe and focused on learning. You have the right to speak out, hand out flyers, and express opinions, as long as it does not disrupt class or violate school rules.

School officials can search your backpack or locker if they have a reasonable suspicion that you broke a rule or the law. However, police officers usually need a warrant or your consent to conduct a search unless there are emergency circumstances.

If you believe your rights are being violated, stay calm, write down what happened, and contact a lawyer or a local ACLU affiliate.

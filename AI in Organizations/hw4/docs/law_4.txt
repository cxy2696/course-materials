Source: Nolo Legal Encyclopedia – Avoiding Misinformation on Social Media

Social media can be a useful way to learn about legal developments, but it is not a substitute for real legal advice. Posts and videos may oversimplify complex laws or leave out key details that apply to your state or case.

Before relying on information from social media, verify it with reliable sources, such as official government websites or licensed attorneys. Laws differ by state, and what is legal in one jurisdiction may not be in another.

Relying on inaccurate online information can lead to serious mistakes in contracts, housing, employment, or criminal matters. Always confirm critical legal information through reputable channels.
